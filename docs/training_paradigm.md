# Training Paradigm

This document is the authoritative planning contract for how STSRL may acquire
training signal. It supplements [`project_architecture.md`](project_architecture.md),
which remains authoritative for simulator, runtime, information-regime, data,
and controller boundaries. External research lineage and publication attribution
requirements are recorded in
[`research_inspirations_and_attribution.md`](research_inspirations_and_attribution.md).

## Objective

The final objective is the probability of defeating the Heart at Ascension 20
from a standard game start under the normal public information available to a
player.

The project pursues simulator-only self-generated policy improvement. Its primary
methodological inspirations are AlphaZero's search-guided tabula-rasa
policy/value improvement and Suphx's training-time Oracle-guiding approach for
imperfect-information learning. STSRL adapts those ideas to a single-agent
stochastic game with hidden future information and long-horizon resource
planning. The citations and the exact boundary between conceptual inspiration
and external implementation reuse are part of the repository's publication
contract; see
[`research_inspirations_and_attribution.md`](research_inspirations_and_attribution.md).

## Human-Knowledge Boundary

The training path does not consume:

- human game trajectories;
- human action labels;
- human deck, card, relic, route, shop, rest, or encounter rankings;
- imitation targets derived from a human player or a human-written expert policy;
- external human win-rate tables as policy supervision.

Repository contributors may use ordinary engineering knowledge to implement the
game interface, legal-action contracts, generic search algorithms, neural
architectures, optimization procedures, diagnostics, and experiment design.
Those engineering choices do not turn human behavior into a learning target.

## Allowed Sources Of Training Signal

Training signal may come from:

1. authoritative simulator rules, legal actions, transitions, and terminal
   outcomes;
2. complete runs generated by current or historical repository controllers;
3. restored checkpoints and simulator-validated counterfactual branches;
4. search visit distributions, action values, leaf values, and terminal returns;
5. training-time full-state Oracle controllers with explicit provenance;
6. simulator-generated assistance, curriculum schedules, or state transforms
   whose distribution identity is preserved;
7. normal-public policies and values trained from the above signals.

All generated rows must retain the behavior controller, target-generating
controller, information regime, distribution kind, simulator identity, and
source identity separately.

## Meaning Of Self-Generated Improvement

Slay the Spire has no adversarial opponent, so the project does not use
`self-play` in the literal two-player sense. The intended loop is
search-guided self-generated policy improvement:

```text
current battle and non-combat policies
    -> simulator trajectories and restored states
    -> search, counterfactual continuation, and Oracle improvement targets
    -> policy/value training
    -> updated policies
    -> changed natural state distribution
```

A larger sample count under an unchanged weak policy only estimates that
policy-induced state distribution more accurately. Scale-up is justified after
an intervention changes reachability, target quality, or training effectiveness.

## Oracle Assistance

Oracle assistance is permitted during training and diagnostics. Every Oracle
artifact must declare `full_simulator_state_oracle_like` or another explicit
privileged regime. Oracle information may support:

- search-improved action distributions;
- action-value or state-value targets;
- hidden-information sensitivity analysis;
- curriculum trajectory discovery;
- transfer to a public-information policy.

Oracle results are not deployment evidence. A deployed controller must consume
only the public decision contract. Until public-consistent hidden-future sampling
exists, a public student distilled from one realized hidden future must report
the resulting teacher ambiguity and may not claim normal-information optimality.

## Bootstrap Behavior Policies

`expert_non_combat_v1` is a seeded stochastic bootstrap exploration policy. Its
purpose is to reach a broader state distribution during early development. The
word `expert` is a historical implementation name and does not grant teacher
status.

Rows generated by a bootstrap heuristic may be used for:

- state visitation and checkpoint collection;
- simulator return and continuation-value targets;
- Oracle relabeling;
- off-policy replay with explicit behavior provenance;
- coverage and failure analysis.

The heuristic action itself must not be treated as a correct-action label for the
final learned non-combat policy. Any future imitation loss must identify a
simulator search or Oracle target independently of the bootstrap behavior action.

## Battle And Non-Combat Separation

Battle and non-combat decisions remain separate modules because their horizons,
action spaces, transition density, and useful search methods differ.

The battle module may use tactical policy priors, tree search, learned leaf
values, chance handling, transposition, and restored-battle curricula. The
non-combat module may use dynamic legal-action ranking, run-level values,
checkpoint continuation, counterfactual action evaluation, and long-horizon
search.

The modules share the final objective through an auditable run-continuation
value. Intermediate battle outcomes such as survival, HP, potions, gold, deck
changes, and relic counters remain separate reportable components and are not
permanently collapsed into fixed human-written reward weights.

## Distribution Contract

The following distributions must remain distinguishable:

- `natural`: standard-start complete runs under named current controllers;
- `oracle_reached`: states reached through a privileged training-time policy;
- `assisted`: states reached under an explicit assistance schedule;
- `transformed`: authoritative states changed by a versioned simulator-validated
  transform;
- `constructed`: explicitly rebuilt supplemental states;
- `stratified` or `resampled`: repeated optimization weight over an existing
  source identity;
- `paired_counterfactual`: matched alternatives from the same source state.

Curriculum and Oracle distributions may train a controller, but final A20
performance claims require standard-start natural evaluation without assistance,
bootstrap heuristics, hidden information, or training-only restart privileges.

## Planning Consequences

The planner must prefer experiments that change or diagnose the generating policy
before approving large fixed-policy collection. A large natural collection is
published only after matched evidence shows that the selected battle/non-combat
policy pair materially improves later-act reachability or that the collection has
a separately justified statistical purpose.

Future learned non-combat work must use simulator returns, counterfactual
continuations, search targets, or Oracle targets rather than imitation of
`expert_non_combat_v1`. Future battle work should move beyond root-only guidance
toward tree-internal policy/value integration when the required simulator surface
is justified by evidence.
