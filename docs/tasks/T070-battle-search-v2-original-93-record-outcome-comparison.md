## Scope

### 1. Fail-closed input and frozen-configuration preflight

Verify the T069 retention manifest, Case A decision, all six locks, T052 cohort,
T043 checkpoint, T068/T061 provenance, exact Python/native ABI pairing, and
pinned source before evaluation.

Write one versioned frozen-configuration report before any outcome aggregation.
It must record:

- baseline budget 100 in every family;
- equal-nominal guided budgets 100/100/100;
- simulator-step guided budgets 86/408/384;
- wall-clock guided budgets 1/1/2;
- `prior_value` as the predeclared primary candidate;
- `prior_only` and `value_only` as mechanism ablations;
- the T069 projection implementation id and complete provenance;
- the exact 93 ordered source identities.

Any identity or lock mismatch fails before evaluation. T070 must not run a new
calibration candidate or change a frozen budget.

### 2. Three matched 93-record comparison families

Evaluate all 93 T052 records, with identical ordering and source identity in
every arm, in exactly these families:

1. equal nominal budget: baseline and all guided arms at native budget 100;
2. simulator-step normalized: baseline 100, `prior_only` 86, `value_only` 408,
   and `prior_value` 384;
3. wall-clock normalized: baseline 100, `prior_only` 1, `value_only` 1, and
   `prior_value` 2.

Every guided arm uses the accepted T069 search-scope projection. Baseline
preserves the accepted T062 `oracle_search_v1`, `highest_mean`, and
`initial_no_potions` behavior. The accepted unprojected scorer path remains
constructible but is not a fourth comparison family or a substitute outcome
arm.

Each family uses 16 explicit contiguous record-range shards covering `0:93`
exactly once and 16 effective workers on the maintainer host. Run families as
separately named stages so actual wall time, native steps, model calls,
failures, and resource use remain attributable.

### 3. Outcome and compute report

For every family and arm, report overall, 88-record Boss-only, and five-record
Act-2+ cells with:

- wins, losses, termination status, and paired win delta;
- terminal absolute current HP and paired HP among outcome ties;
- structured battle-end resources and potion outcome;
- selected occurrence-safe root action and first selected-action divergence;
- native and outer simulator steps, model calls, search budgets, projection
  construction/reuse counts, and wall-clock seconds;
- restore, action-mapping, checkpoint, missing-value, fallback, controller,
  truncation, and mixed-provenance failures, including explicit zero counts;
- deterministic paired bootstrap 95% confidence intervals.

Keep the three comparison families separate. Do not replace measured cost with
the T069 calibration ratios, combine favorable outcomes across arms/families,
or describe the wall-clock family as equal compute when its actual measured
cost differs.

### 4. Decision

Apply the original T062 decision boundary without retuning it.
`prior_value` remains the predeclared Search v2 candidate; `prior_only` and
`value_only` remain mechanism ablations.

Recommend exactly one bounded Search v2 complete-run evaluation task only when
all of these gates pass:

- zero restore, action-mapping, checkpoint, missing-value, fallback,
  controller, truncation, or mixed-provenance failures;
- equal-nominal `prior_value` has positive paired win delta overall, bootstrap
  95% CI lower bound at least zero, and no negative win delta in either
  stratum;
- `prior_value` has no negative paired win delta overall or by either stratum
  in both compute-normalized families and a positive overall win delta in at
  least one of them;
- mean paired terminal HP among outcome-tied records is non-negative in every
  family;
- actual simulator-step-normalized primary cost is within 5% of baseline and
  actual wall-clock-normalized primary cost is within 10%.

If any gate fails, do not promote the controller or continue cost repair.
Recommend exactly one evidence-based algorithm/data repair or closure from the
observed prior/value ablations and outcome failures. The recommendation may
not be another cache, projection, feature-copy, tensor/callback optimization,
budget calibration, or differently named continuation of the T067--T069 cost
line.
